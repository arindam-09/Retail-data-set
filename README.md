# Retail-data-set
Page 1 (Q1) — Revenue by Month
A *line chart* tracking monthly revenue across the full year. Key highlights:
- Revenue starts at *0.69M* in January, dips in February (0.52M), then gradually climbs
- A strong peak in *November at 1.51M*, making it the best month
- Sharp drop in December to *0.64M*
- Shows clear *Q4 seasonality*, with the biggest growth from September onward



 Page 2 (Q2) — Revenue & Quantity by Country
A *horizontal bar chart* comparing Sum of Revenue vs. Sum of Quantity per country:
- *Netherlands* leads in revenue (285K) with 201K quantity
- *EIRE* (Ireland) is second at 283K revenue / 147K quantity
- *Germany* and *France* follow closely
- Also includes Australia, Spain, Switzerland, Belgium, Sweden, and Japan
- Shows which markets generate high revenue vs. high volume



 Page 3 (Q3) — Revenue by CustomerID
A *bar chart* showing revenue contribution by individual customers:
- Top customer (~15K ID range) generates *280K* in revenue
- Another high-value customer at ~18K generates *260K*
- Useful for identifying *VIP/key accounts* and revenue concentration risk


Page 4 (Q4) — Quantity by Country (Map)
An *interactive bubble map* showing quantity distribution worldwide:
- Largest bubbles concentrated in *Western Europe* (France, Germany, Netherlands, EIRE)
- Smaller presence in *Australia, Japan, Hong Kong, Singapore, UAE*
- Minimal activity in *USA, Brazil, Canada, and RSA*
- Visually confirms Europe as the *primary market*



*Tools used: Microsoft Power BI Desktop
*Key metrics: Revenue, Quantity
*Dimensions: Month, Country, CustomerID
